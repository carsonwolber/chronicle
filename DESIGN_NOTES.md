# Visual direction proposal

Replace the light editorial mockup with a dark, compact daily-game interface: graphite surfaces, high-contrast text, lime accents, dense event rows, and a clear daily/free-play status.

Reference cues are the daily identity and spoiler-safe share output associated with [Wordle](https://www.nytimes.com/games/wordle/index.html), the daily-plus-unlimited structure in [Globle](https://globle.game/how-to-play), and the compact clue rows in [Countryle](https://countryle.com/). Chronicle keeps its own event-ordering mechanic and visual identity.

This is the first layer of a stacked demo. The page references game logic and puzzle data that arrive in later PRs. Review the visual treatment after the full stack is available.
